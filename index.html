Hello, I'm Kiarash

I study how agents learn reusable structure: skills, programs and models of the world.

I'm a PhD student in Computing Science at the University of Alberta, advised by Levi Lelis. I work in reinforcement learning, on problems where structure helps an agent generalize: discovering options for hierarchical RL, representing policies as programs, and using LLMs as world models.

Before my PhD I was a support researcher at Huawei in Edmonton for two years, working on image-quality assessment, ISP and compiler optimization. I did my MSc at UAlberta with Martin Müller, where I studied Monte Carlo Tree Search with imperfect models, and my BSc in Computer Engineering at Iran University of Science and Technology.
